**Project:** PulseCam – Contactless Heart Rate Estimation from Facial Video (student research prototype)
**Team / contact:** ______________________  **Guide:** ______________________

**Purpose.** We are testing whether a webcam can estimate heart rate from the face, and how accurate it is for different skin tones. PulseCam is a learning project. **It is not a medical device and gives no medical advice.** If you are worried about your heart, please see a doctor.

**What will be recorded.** Four 1-minute videos of your face (sitting still, slow head movement, reading aloud, dim light), plus your heart rate from a pulse oximeter / smartwatch / manual wrist count. We will also note your age group, whether you wear glasses, and your skin shade on the Monk scale. Your name is **not** used in file names; you are given an ID (e.g. P01).

**How it is stored.** Videos and this form are kept in a private team drive, shared only with the project team. They are never posted publicly or on GitHub. The list linking your ID to your name is kept in a separate private file.

**How it is used.** To train/test the model, measure accuracy, and write the project report. Your face will appear in the report, slides or demo **only if you tick the box below**.
