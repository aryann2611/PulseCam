Read before quoting a number

  • The deep models are rPPG-Toolbox's released checkpoints. A fine-tune on UBFC gave no gain, so nothing trained for this project beats them yet.
  • UBFC is indoor, still and uncompressed. Real webcam video will give larger errors.
  • TS-CAN trained on SCAMPS transfers poorly to UBFC. This was checked with 5 scoring methods, including the toolbox's own, so it is not a scoring mistake.
  • Robustness to movement and light (E3), skin-tone bands (E4) and the live comparison with a reference device (E5) need volunteer recordings and have no results yet.
